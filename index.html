Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in your web design packages?

Our comprehensive packages include custom design, responsive development, SEO optimization, hosting for 12 months, SSL security, and 3 months of post-launch support. Every project also includes detailed analytics setup and training.

How long does it take to complete a website?

Most projects are completed within 4-6 weeks. Simple websites may take 2-3 weeks, while complex e-commerce sites can take 8-12 weeks. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance and support?

Yes, we offer flexible maintenance packages starting from $99/month, including regular updates, security monitoring, content updates, and technical support. Emergency support is available 24/7.

Can you help with content creation?

Absolutely! We offer professional copywriting, photography, and video production services to ensure your website has high-quality, engaging content that converts visitors into customers.

What payment terms do you offer?

We offer flexible payment plans with a 50% deposit to commence work, and the remaining balance split into milestones. We accept all major credit cards, bank transfers, and PayPal.
